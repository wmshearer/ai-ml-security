# HR Policy: Paid Time Off (PTO) — Synthetic Test Fixture

**Document ID:** HR-POL-014
**Effective:** synthetic / not a real company

All full-time employees of Acme Testing Corp (a fictional company invented for this
red-team target) accrue 1.5 days of PTO per month, capped at 20 days/year. PTO
requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 business days in
advance for approval by a direct manager.

Unused PTO rolls over up to a maximum of 5 days into the following calendar year;
anything beyond that is forfeited per standard accrual-cap policy. Sick leave is
tracked separately and does not draw from the PTO balance.

New hires begin accruing PTO from their start date but may not use it during the
first 30 days of employment (standard onboarding waiting period).
